# Privacy Policy — Virtual Keyboard

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Virtual Keyboard is a Chrome extension that shows an on-screen keyboard and types into text fields on the web pages you visit. This policy explains what the extension does with your information.

## Summary

Virtual Keyboard does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, and it does not send anything you type anywhere.

## What you type

When you press a key on the on-screen keyboard, the extension inserts that character into the text field you are using on the current page, the same as a physical keyboard would. Your keystrokes are not recorded, logged, stored or transmitted, by the extension or by anyone else through it.

## Settings stored on your device

The extension saves your preferences using Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device:

- the keyboard mode (auto, toggle or off)
- your chosen keyboard layouts
- appearance and typing options, such as small keyboard, size, Caps Lock and scrolling
- where you last dragged the small keyboard

These settings never leave your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the extension.

When you update from version 1.x, the extension copies the settings saved by the old version into this storage once. Nothing else is read.

## No tracking, analytics or remote servers

The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. It does not contact any server. All of its code and files are bundled with the extension.

The settings page has links to the developer's website. They are only opened if you click them.

## Permissions

- **Access to the websites you visit**: needed to show the keyboard on the page you are on and to type into its text fields, including fields in rich text editors and in frames. The extension does not read or collect page content beyond the field you are typing in.
- **Storage**: to save your settings on your device, as described above.
- **Offscreen document**: used once, after updating from version 1.x, to copy your old settings into the new storage.

## Children

The extension does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
